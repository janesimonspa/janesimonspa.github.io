Privacy policy

Jane, a private personal assistant · last updated October 10, 2026

Who uses Jane

Jane is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public. It connects to one Google account, jane.simons.pa@gmail.com.

What Google data Jane uses, and why

  • Gmail: to read and sort mail sent or forwarded to Jane's own address, tell the owner what needs attention, file newsletters into labels, and prepare draft replies. Jane sends an email only after the owner approves it.
  • Google Calendar: to see free and busy times, add and change events the owner asks for, and count meetings per day (numbers only) for the owner's personal health notes.
  • Google Drive and Sheets: only the spreadsheets Jane creates itself, such as the owner's expense ledger and research sheets, shared with the owner.

Where the data goes

Jane runs on the owner's own computer. Google data is fetched only to do the task the owner asked for. Notes Jane keeps are stored in an encrypted vault on that computer. The text needed for a task is processed by the AI model Jane runs on (Anthropic's Claude) to carry out that task.

Google user data is never sold, never shared with advertisers or data brokers, never used for advertising, and never used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Jane's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

The owner can remove Jane's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all further access. Data Jane stored on the owner's computer is deleted at the owner's request.
