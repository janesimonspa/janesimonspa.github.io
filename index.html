Jane

A private personal assistant for one person.

Jane is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer and talks to its owner through Telegram. It helps with notes, reminders, email triage, the calendar, research and personal spreadsheets.

Jane is not a public service: it has one user, its owner, and it is not offered to anyone else. It connects only to its own Google account, jane.simons.pa@gmail.com, which the owner forwards email to and shares calendars with.
